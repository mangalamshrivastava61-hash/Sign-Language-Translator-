How It Works

Your camera stream is parsed live into 21 high-dimensional 3D skeletal hand landmarks via MediaPipe. Features are normalized around the wrist joint to ensure invariant tracking across scale, distance, and tilt.

An ensemble Random Forest model evaluates coordinate vector topologies in real time. Steady gestures (~0.5s stability) are auto-committed to your sentence stream, or you can commit on-demand with Space.
